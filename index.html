How the stand works

Self-serve, on the honor system

You stop in, pick what you want and pay at the stand. That trust is what makes a little stand like this work.

  1. Pull in

    Find the wooden stand and the Kranky Chicken Farm Stand sign at 13687 Woodworth Road, New Springfield.

  2. Pick your goods

    Check the shelves, the baskets and the fridge for eggs and whatever came out of the garden that week.

  3. Pay at the stand

    Prices are on the handwritten cards. Self-pay right there at the stand.

  4. Need more?

    Please message us, I'm sure we can offer more than what's in the stand. Call or text (253) 278-7534.
